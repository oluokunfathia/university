FROM THE CEO'S DESK

From the
CEO's Desk

A renewed commitment to keep raising the bar.

Dear Valued Customer,

It gives me great pleasure to welcome you aboard your Ibom Air flight. Thank you for choosing us. This is my very first message to you as MD/CEO and I am delighted to introduce the 17th edition of Falcon, your trusted in-flight companion.

Three months ago, I was granted the great privilege of assuming the reins of this remarkable company, when our founding CEO and the arrowhead of our vision and strategy that shaped our success story, Mr. Mfon Udom, retired.

I am deeply humbled by the opportunity to become an integral part of this effort from the beginning. I am still deeply touched by the opportunity.

As at today, 6 years down the line, Ibom Air has operated more than 54,000 scheduled commercial flights, carrying more than 4 million passengers safely.

These numbers tell a story of trust. They also demonstrate our unwavering commitment to getting you where you want to be, on time and with the excellent service you deserve.

While we are very proud of the success we have achieved so far, I want all of you to know that the entire Ibom Air team is very much aware of the huge responsibility that our success comes with.

The responsibility to consistently meet your now very high expectations. And I promise on behalf of the team that we will continue to strive to meet your expectations always and do this through creativity, innovation and an uncompromising commitment to safety.

Meanwhile, this edition, themed "Into the Delta", will take you on a journey into one of Nigeria's resource-rich regions, the Niger Delta.

Our cover story spotlights the Niger Delta Development Commission and offers insights into the region and its future.

Beyond the cover story, you will find thoughtful, curated content to keep you entertained, informed and inspired from destinations we explore to conversations that challenge new ideas.

I hope you enjoy every page of this edition, and please feel free to take this copy with you when you disembark.

As always, we remain committed to making every journey with Ibom Air exceptional.

Thank you once again for flying with us.

George Ure CEO
