RUTGERS UNIVERSITY-CAMDEN

Rutgers University is one of the oldest, most respected research universities in America. At our Camden campus, you'll find all of the expert faculty and innovative academic programs that you'd expect from such an illustrious institution, nurtured in a supportive, welcoming community at the center of one of the most exciting metropolitan regions in the country.

We're proud to provide our diverse student body with personalized support to ensure that everyone can achieve their academic ambitions. With a degree from Rutgers University-Camden, you'll be ready to pursue your future career wherever it might take you.
